PROJECT DESCRIPTION

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

This is a university management portal, think of the university as a huge building.
inside that building, we have;

. students
. lecturers
. non-academic staff
. administrators 
. departments
. faculties
. courses
. academic sessions
. semesters
. results
. payments
. admissions
. documents
. notifcations

This software is basically a digital version of that entire university administration system.
The people using this won't all see the same thing. So basically there will be different UI/UX, roles etc.
Example;

Student - see > student portal
Lecturer - see > lecturer portal
Admin - see > admin portal
and above all of them is the SUPER Admin

    THE VARIOUS PORTALS

SUPER ADMIN PORTAL (MOST IMPORTANT PORTAL)

Who is the super admin? 
Imagine someone sitting in the university's central administrative office.
The person can control almost everything in the system.

The super admin can:

. create administrators
. create faculties
. create departments
. create programs
. create courses
. create academin sessions
. create semesters
. create users
. create permissions
. view systen activities
. configure the portal

So yeah basically the super admin portal is the control room of the university portal.

22. Audit Logs

This is extremely important for your Super Admin system.

Imagine someone changes a student's result.

You need to know:

WHO did it?
WHAT did they change?
WHEN did they do it?
WHICH record did they change?

So you record:

Actor:
Admin 123

Action:
RESULT_UPDATED

Target:
Student 456

Time:
2026-09-02 08:30

Result:
Success

This gives you accountability.
